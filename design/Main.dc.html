Why Bayrust

Proof, not opinions

Every finding is a tagged part

An ID, a lens, a severity, replayable evidence and a specific fix. Nothing vague, nothing to argue about.

Tested where your users are

Low-cost Android phones, slow and dropping networks, shared devices, mobile money and USSD. The conditions your product actually lives in.

Written for two readers

Engineers get reproduction steps, delivered straight into Jira, GitHub, Trello or a spreadsheet. Leaders get one page on what to fund first.

Independent by design

We don’t build software or sell tools, so there’s no follow-on contract to protect. The report says what’s true, not what’s convenient.

The re-check is included

Once your team ships the fixes, we inspect every one again and confirm the rust is really gone, with fresh evidence.

When teams call us

You don’t need a crisis to need an audit

The teams closest to a product are the last to see its rust. An outside inspection is worth it at four moments.

Before a launch

The cheapest moment to find rust is before your customers do. We can inspect prototypes, staging and beta builds.

Before you accept a vendor’s delivery

An independent check that what you paid for actually works, before the final invoice is signed.

When customers complain and nobody knows why

Support calls are rising, completion is falling, and the team can’t agree on the cause. We find it.

Once a year, as a health check

Products drift as features pile up. A yearly inspection catches the rust before it spreads.
