Anatomy of a finding

Every finding has the same six parts

ID and lens

A unique reference and the lens it was found through, so every finding can be tracked to its fix.

Severity

How much harm it causes and how many people it affects. It sets the order of work.

Evidence

A screen recording, screenshot or log that anyone can replay. No finding without proof.

Steps to reproduce

The exact device, network and actions that trigger it, so your engineers see it first time.

Recommended fix

A specific change your team can implement, written for your stack.

Re-check status

Open, fixed or confirmed. Updated after the re-check with fresh evidence.

Severity

A severity that sets the order of work

Critical

Blocks a core task or causes harm: lost money, exposed data, people locked out. Fix before anything else.

Major

Causes real failure or confusion for many users, with a painful workaround at best.

Minor

Friction or inconsistency that slows people down but doesn’t stop them.

Passed

Inspected and working as intended. Recorded so you know what we checked.
